Abass Abiola Abdul-Azeez
Lead Facilitator

Abass Abiola Abdul-Azeez is a learning and development expert driven by the belief that “when people know better, they perform better.”

Over the past seven years, he has designed and delivered transformative learning experiences for audiences ranging from students to C-Suite executives.

An award-winning writer, public speaker, and published author. Abass has contributed to communications and knowledge-sharing initiatives for global organisations including Space Generation Advisory Council and several TEDx platforms home and abroad.

His work is grounded in neuroscience-backed teaching methods, particularly Brain Compatible Learning (BCL). Beyond facilitation, he enjoys digital illustration, storytelling, movies on business and career growth, and long walks with nature.

Yusuf Adua
Head of Strategy
Yusuf Adua is an Arabic, German, and (he thinks) English-speaking narrative strategist. He is a non-violent communicator passionate about amplifying ignored but essential stories, ideas, and human potential.
With a background in practical, skill-building education, human-interest journalism, personality psychology, business storytelling, and organisational management, he helps shape learning experiences and strategic initiatives that enable individuals and organisations engaging with TheSIS to recognise, develop, and apply their strengths to the fullest.
"The Wizard" has worked with both national and international organisations, enjoys raising money for nonprofits, and, in his free time, relaxes with the inside stories of professional football.

Oluwaseun Ojo Esq.
Secretary

Oluwaseun Ojo is a young legal professional and development enthusiast passionate about leadership, human capacity development, and problem-solving.

He currently serves as an Associate at Queens Attorneys LP and as Compliance Officer/Secretary at The School for Ignored Skills Ltd/Gte (TheSIS).

He has held leadership roles including Director of Communications for YALI Network Kwara, Speaker of the Quantum Leap Students Assembly, and Editor-in-Chief of the Lawrit Journal of Law.

Oluwaseun has facilitated legal research bootcamps and published opinion articles on platforms including Punch Newspapers and Vanguard Media.
